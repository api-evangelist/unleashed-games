# Unleashed Games

> Unleashed Games is an independent game studio founded by veterans of World of Warcraft, Medal of Honor, and Monopoly Go!, backed by a16z Speedrun. Its flagship title Haven is a cross-platform co-op RPG for families and friends (PC, Mac, console, mobile) built around procedurally generated maps, village building, and collaborative combat. The studio publishes no public developer platform, API, or SDK today.
